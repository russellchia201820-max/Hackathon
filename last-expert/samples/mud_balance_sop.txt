STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: MUD DENSITY USING A MUD BALANCE

Purpose
To measure the density (mud weight) of drilling fluid.

Equipment
Mud balance with cup, lid, beam, rider and level bubble. Fresh water for calibration.

Procedure
1. Place the mud balance base on a flat, level surface.
2. Fill the clean, dry cup with a fresh sample of the mud to be tested.
3. Place the lid on the cup and seat it firmly with a twisting motion. Some mud will be expelled through the hole in the lid.
4. Cover the hole with a finger and wash or wipe the outside of the cup and arm clean and dry.
5. Place the balance arm on the base, with the knife edge resting on the fulcrum.
6. Move the rider until the level bubble is centred.
7. Read the density at the edge of the rider closest to the cup.
8. Record the mud weight in pounds per gallon (ppg).

Calibration
Check the balance periodically with fresh water. It should read 8.33 ppg at 70 degrees F. Adjust using the calibration screw or lead shot as required.
